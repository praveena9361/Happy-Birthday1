Chaotic Memory

I will never forget the day I joined 8th grade in a new school. I wasn’t able to speak to new people because everyone was new to me, so I stayed quiet. After a week, some people approached me, and I started talking to them. Then I met a person — she approached me and said “Hi.” I never knew that day that she was going to become my best friend. We started talking and sharing a lot of things, and slowly we became good friends. One day, she introduced me to her gang members, and I became close to them, especially to Mouni. Then a bad thing happened. On my 13th birthday, I was completely shocked, and I didn’t know how to handle the situation. But suddenly, that incident changed everything. That birthday is something I will never forget. I hope you still remember what happened to us that day. Then lockdown arrived. We couldn’t talk much in that time, but we stayed connected through mobile phones. Later, we went back to school in 10th standard. We shared even more secrets and became closer. We even fought for marks sometimes, but we never felt jealous of each other. We were understanding and remained good friends during that time. After completing 10th standard, we moved to 11th standard and chose the same course — Computer Science. At that time, she started ignoring me because she had a new friend. I felt really sad because of that. Later, I became close to someone else too, but I never forgot to talk to her. Every morning, I would say hi or give her a compliment. I kept forcing conversations until one day I realized I was only saying “hi” and “bye.” After 12th standard began, we joined the same tuition center, and once again we started talking like the old days. Then some conflicts happened in tuition, but after completing 12th standard, we became super close friends again because of the lessons life taught us. You know everything that happened between us, Mouni. Thank you for always being by my side. Happy Birthday, my dearrrr Mouniiiii ! 🤍
